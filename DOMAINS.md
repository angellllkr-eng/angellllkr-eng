# Domain Registry

Only domains with a real public purpose should be promoted.

| Asset | Canonical domain | Purpose | SEO role |
|---|---|---|---|
| MindReply | `mind-reply.com` | commercial product | primary brand/search identity |

## Rules

- Prefer a canonical domain over many thin subdomains.
- Keep one canonical URL per public page and redirect variants.
- Use descriptive page titles, metadata, Open Graph, structured data where truthful, sitemap, robots policy, and Search Console verification.
- Never create doorway pages or keyword-stuffed names merely for ranking.
- Internal/private surfaces do not need public SEO indexing.
- Record domain ownership, DNS provider, TLS, renewal, and deployment target in the private estate inventory.

Future domains require a written purpose, audience, canonical content plan, and ownership record before registration or launch.
