# Estate Profile

The public profile is the front door. It should explain outcomes without exposing private operating detail.

## Public-facing identity

**Signalcraft** — evidence-led engineering for products that need to be useful, observable and recoverable.

## Portfolio map

- **Proofline** — canonical product and release evidence.
- **Crownline** — private operations and owner approval.
- **Dialogue Atlas** — owner-facing conversation surface.

## Profile rule

Show what a visitor can understand and verify. Keep private architecture, credentials, personal context and operational secrets out of the public profile.

## SEO vocabulary

Use concrete terms naturally: `Next.js`, `TypeScript`, `Python`, `Supabase`, `Postgres`, `Stripe`, `GitHub Actions`, `automation`, `observability`, `release engineering`, `mobile-first SaaS`, `product design`.

Do not stuff brand terms where a normal technical phrase is clearer.
